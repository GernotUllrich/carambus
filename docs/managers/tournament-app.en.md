# Tournament App

For tournament directors who run a tournament with the tournament app, and for club admins who provide it.

## What the tournament app is {#was}

The tournament app is part of Carambus. It runs in the browser (laptop or tablet) and is served by your own Carambus
server under `/app/`. It runs tournaments alongside the [tournament monitor](tournament-management.md) — formats it
does not cover (the 3-cushion team championship, knockout tournaments in pool and snooker) and tournaments running at
the same time as another one at the same venue — and places the matches on the Carambus scoreboards. Players play and
enter scores at the table as usual; the app fetches the results from the scoreboard. Which way applies to a tournament
is covered by [Tournament monitor or tournament app?](tournament-management.md#monitor-or-app).

The app's interface is German; the button labels below are quoted as they appear, with a translation.

| Format | Purpose | Tried in a real tournament |
|--------|---------|----------------------------|
| **TournamentPlan (T01–T24)** | Carambus tournament plans with groups and final round; attaches to a Carambus tournament | yes — CEB Ladies tournament, BC Wedel, 21–23 Aug 2026 |
| **KO system (singles)** | Single and double elimination following the ClubCloud plans, also for 8-, 9-, 10-ball, 14.1 straight and snooker | no |
| **3-cushion team championship** | 4 teams × 4 players | no |
| **League match day** | Team match day following the league schedule, results by direct entry or scoreboard | no |

The interface behind it is described in the [External Tournament Bridge](external-tournament-bridge.md).

### Which case? {#fall}

| Your tournament | Way in the app | Guide |
|-----------------|----------------|-------|
| Carom tournament with a Carambus tournament plan (T01–T24), created in Carambus as an app tournament | „🔗 → Plan-Turnier attachen“ (attach plan tournament) | [Plan tournament](#anlegen) — tried in a real tournament |
| Pool or snooker tournament from the ClubCloud (in Carambus with a registration list) | „🏆 → KO-Turnier anlegen“ (create KO tournament) | [KO tournament from the ClubCloud](#ko-aus-clubcloud) — not tried yet |

Whether a tournament belongs in the app at all is covered by
[Tournament monitor or tournament app?](tournament-management.md#monitor-or-app)

### Where the data lives {#daten}

- **The tournament state** — seeding list, recorded results, standings — lives in the browser of the device running
  the app. Carambus knows the plan, the seeding list, the bound tables and the match currently on each table.
- **The result archive:** in the plan, KO and 3-cushion team formats the app additionally stores the final standings
  with the matches in Carambus after every result; they appear on the tournament page. You can also do this by hand
  at any time with „📦 Ergebnisse in Carambus ablegen“ (store results in Carambus).
- **Nothing comes back** from the archive into the app. If the tournament state in the browser is lost, you need the
  [backup](#fuehren).

## Prerequisites (club admin) {#voraussetzungen}

1. **Enable serving:** `serve_tournament_app: true` in `carambus_data/scenarios/<scenario>/config.yml`. The app files
   ship with every Carambus deploy; the switch takes effect through the nginx configuration and the `carambus.yml`,
   both generated by `rake "scenario:prepare_deploy[<scenario>]"`. Without it, the server answers `/app/` with 404 and
   the tournament page does not show the [button](#link). The button is also missing as long as a server's
   `carambus.yml` has not been regenerated.
2. **Create the service account:** the app signs in with a service account — a user in the database of the server
   it is served from. Create it in that server's deploy directory:

    ```bash
    cd /var/www/<basename>/current
    RAILS_ENV=production bundle exec rake "service_accounts:create_carambus_app[<SHORTNAME>]"
    ```

    The short name forms the address `carambus-app-<shortname>-bridge@carambus.de`, for example the club server's or
    the region's short name; this address goes into the [link](#link). The password is printed only once; pass it on
    to the tournament directors securely. The same call prefixed with
    `ROTATE=1` creates a new password and invalidates existing sign-ins. Details:
    [External Tournament Bridge, step 1](external-tournament-bridge.md#step-1-create-the-service-account-sportwart).

## Creating a plan tournament (club admin) {#anlegen}

The tournament belongs in Carambus, not in the app: the plan, seeding list and table bindings live there, which is
why several devices can work on it. Three settings matter:

- **`manual_assignment`** must be set, otherwise Carambus runs the plan itself and gets in the app's way.
- **An end date in the future** — otherwise the [nightly run](#aufraeumen) removes the tournament.
- **Region** and **tournament plan** — without a region the app cannot find the tournament, without a plan it cannot
  take it over.

!!! warning "The web form does not work for this today"
    The form under „Neues Turnier“ (new tournament) does not store a region; the app cannot find a tournament
    created that way. On top of that, the web interface only creates the tournament monitor with „Starte den
    Turnier-Monitor“ (start the tournament monitor) — and that creates the plan's games, which collide with the app.
    Until this is fixed, an admin creates the tournament in the server's Rails console.

In the console (`cd /var/www/<basename>/current && RAILS_ENV=production bundle exec rails c`):

```ruby
region = Region.find_by!(shortname: "<REGION>")
t = Tournament.create!(
  title: "<title>", shortname: "<short name>",
  date:     Time.zone.parse("<YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM>"),
  end_date: Time.zone.parse("<YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM>"),   # in the future: protects against the nightly run
  organizer: region, region_id: region.id, season: Season.current_season,
  location_id: <location id>, discipline_id: <discipline id>,
  tournament_plan: TournamentPlan.find_by!(name: "<e.g. T08>"),
  balls_goal: <distance>, innings_goal: <innings>,
  manual_assignment: true,                           # the app runs the plan, not Carambus
  data: {}
)

# seeding list in seed order
Player.find([<player ids>]).each_with_index { |p, i| t.seedings.create!(player: p, position: i + 1) }

t.initialize_tournament_monitor
t.reload
t.games.destroy_all   # the plan games created with the monitor — the app creates its own

puts "id=#{t.id} monitor=#{t.tournament_monitor&.id} seedings=#{t.seedings.count} games=#{t.games.count}"
```

The output must end with `games=0`. Games are created later, one by one, when the app places them on the tables.

**Players who do not exist yet** (such as guests without a DBU number) are found by first and last name. Created with
a nationality, scoreboard and app show the country flag:

```ruby
Player.create!(firstname: "<first name>", lastname: "<last name>",
               nationality: "BE",               # ISO country code, gives the flag
               international_player: true, region_id: region.id)
```

Players already in the database are reused, not created again.

## The button and the link {#link}

On the tournament page in Carambus, **"Open in tournament app"** opens the app in a new
tab with region and tournament filled in. If the server has exactly one service account for the app, it is already in
the sign-in field; only the password is missing. The button is shown to admins, the tournament's director and sports
officers whose scope covers the tournament — if the server serves the app ([prerequisites](#voraussetzungen)). It is
missing

- if the tournament has no region — the app would not find it;
- while the tournament monitor runs the tournament (the choice has been made; an app tournament has
  `manual_assignment` set);
- for tournaments with results from the ClubCloud.

Without the button, with several service accounts on the server, or for another device, the same link takes the
tournament director into the app:

```text
https://<carambus-server>/app/?cb_region=<REGION>&cb_email=<service account>&cb_tournament_id=<tournament id>
```

| Part | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `/app/` | the app on your own Carambus server; the trailing slash belongs to it |
| `cb_region` | the tournament's region |
| `cb_email` | the service account. Without it a different account is in the sign-in field and signing in fails |
| `cb_tournament_id` | the tournament number in Carambus — the only part that changes for another tournament |

The password is never part of the link. The app derives the server address from the browser address; `cb_base_url`
is only needed when the app is served from a different server than Carambus.

## Running a plan tournament {#fuehren}

1. On the tournament page **"Open in tournament app"** (or the [link](#link)), enter the
   service account's password.
2. On the overview choose **„🔗 → Plan-Turnier attachen“** (attach plan tournament) — not „➕ Neues Turnier“ (new
   tournament): that would create a second tournament and book the tables twice.
3. **„▶ Turnier laden“** (load tournament): the app fetches plan, seeding list and distance from Carambus and binds
   the tables to the tournament.
4. In the „Spiele & Gruppen“ tab (games & groups) **„▶ Freie Tische belegen“** (fill free tables): the first round
   starts, the matches appear on the scoreboards.
5. After every match **„📥 Ergebnis holen“** (fetch result): the app takes over the result entered at the scoreboard
   and releases the table.
6. **Back up after every round:** „Endstand“ tab (final standings), „Sicherung“ card (backup) → „💾 Sicherung
   speichern“ (save backup, laptop) or „📋 Sicherung kopieren“ (copy backup) and paste it into a note (tablet).

!!! warning "Why the backup is mandatory"
    The tournament state lives in the browser. If the browser clears its storage — Safari on the iPad does so under
    memory pressure without warning — it is gone, and the app does not fetch it back from Carambus. The backup is the
    only safeguard; it takes ten seconds.

### Releasing and re-binding tables {#tische-loesen}

When not all tables are needed towards the end of a tournament, the tournament director releases them one by one
for club play: in the table bar („Spiele & Gruppen“) every **free** table carries an **✕**. After confirming, the
table is no longer bound to the tournament, disappears from the tournament overview and stays greyed out as
„gelöst“ (released) in the bar. **„⟲ einbinden“** brings it back so it can be played on again.

An occupied table has no ✕ — finish the game and fetch the result, or withdraw the call („Ansetzen zurücknehmen“)
first. Carambus itself refuses to release a table while a result on it has not been fetched yet, and says why.

In the correct state the „Setup“ tab shows **„🔗 Attached an Carambus-Turnier #&lt;tournament id&gt;“**, and the top
right reads „Carambus: verbunden“ (connected).

## Handing over to another tournament director {#uebergabe}

The link alone gives your colleague the plan, seeding list and tables, but not the results recorded so far. A handover
therefore consists of:

1. **the link**, unchanged,
2. **the password** — separately, not through the same channel,
3. **the backup** as a file or copied text.

Your colleague imports the backup on the overview with **„Sicherung wiederherstellen“** (restore backup), then opens
the link and signs in. Then they open the restored tournament — **do not attach again**: that clears the recorded
results in the app. If the app reports „Table not bound to this tournament“, use „🔗 Tische neu binden“ (rebind
tables) in the „Setup“ tab.

Any number of devices may follow along. Only one should start matches, otherwise two matches end up on the same
table; who is in charge is an agreement, not a lock in the system.

## KO tournament from the ClubCloud (pool, snooker) {#ko-aus-clubcloud}

For a pool or snooker tournament that came into Carambus from the ClubCloud, the app takes over the registration list
and creates its **own** KO tournament from it. The ClubCloud tournament in Carambus stays untouched; tables, matches and
the result archive belong to the tournament the app creates. This path exists in the code but has not been tried in a
real tournament yet.

1. **Check the registration list:** the app takes over the registration list that is in Carambus; the button does not
   fetch it again. If you ask the Carambus assistant („öffne das Turnier … in der Turnier-App“), you get the link with
   the registration list freshly fetched from the ClubCloud.
2. **Open the app:** on the tournament page **"Open in tournament app"**, or via the
   [link](#link) with the tournament number from the address of the tournament page, `…/tournaments/<tournament id>`.
3. **Sign in.** The overview shows the box „🔗 Aus dem Carambus-Chat vorbereitet“ (prepared from the Carambus chat) with
   tournament name, discipline and number of players.
4. **„🏆 → KO-Turnier anlegen“** (create KO tournament) — the registration list becomes the seeding list. Not „Plan-Turnier
   attachen“: that only applies to tournaments with a Carambus tournament plan.
5. In the „Setup“ tab: choose the **„KO-Plan (ClubCloud)“** matching the number of players (single or double
   elimination), set the **discipline** (e.g. 9-ball) and the targets, **„📥 Tische aus Carambus laden“** (load tables
   from Carambus) and select the tables, then **„🏁 Turnier anlegen & Bracket erzeugen“** (create tournament & bracket).
6. In the „Spiele & Bracket“ tab (games & bracket) choose a table per match and **„▶ Starten“** (start) — the match
   appears on the scoreboard. After the match **„📥 Ergebnis holen“** (fetch result), or „✎ Ergebnis erfassen“ (enter
   result) by hand.

**If the preview shows 0 players**, the registration list is missing in Carambus: fetch it fresh from the ClubCloud via
the assistant, or choose the players in the setup with „📥 Clubs laden“ (load clubs) → club → player.

!!! warning "Limits of the KO path"
    - **No backup:** the KO format has no backup button; the tournament state lives only in the browser. After every
      result the app stores the final standings in the result archive in Carambus.
    - **Not back into the ClubCloud:** the app uploads nothing to the ClubCloud. In the „Endstand“ tab it exports the
      final ranking in ClubCloud format („📄 Rangliste (ClubCloud)“) and the matches („📄 Spiele-CSV“); the tournament
      director makes the entry in the ClubCloud.

## DBU Grand Prix Biathlon {#gp-biathlon}

For the DBU Grand Prix Biathlon (tender "Teilungsliste 4 Billards", 10–32 starters) the plan scheme
has its own mode. The plan is built only at the start from the number of starters — late entries
before the registration deadline just shift the group split. The game runs as the app's **own
tournament**, not attached to the DBU tournament in Carambus.

1. **Open the app** and establish the **Carambus connection** at the top right (green). In the box
   **"Neues Turnier"** choose the scheme **"🗂 TournamentPlan (T01–T24)"**, enter a title (e.g.
   "23. DBU Grand Prix Biathlon"), press **"Turnier anlegen"** and open the tournament under
   "Turniere". The box "Offene Meldelisten (ClubCloud)" does not help here — it only shows
   ClubCloud registration lists of your own region.
2. Setup, card **"1 · Turnierplan"**: **"🎯 DBU Grand Prix Biathlon"**.
3. Card **"2 · Teilnehmer"**: enter the **Carambus-Turnier-Nr.** of the DBU tournament (the number
   in the address of the tournament page, `…/tournaments/<no.>`) and **"📥 Meldeliste
   übernehmen"**. Taking it over again after the deadline **replaces** the list.
4. **Build the seeding list:** the registration list is not a seeding list. Under **"Setzliste aus
   der Deutschen Rangliste Biathlon"** paste the text of the DBU ranking (PDF) and press **"↕
   Setzliste aus Rangliste bilden"**. Registered players with a rank come first by place, everyone
   without a rank after them in registration order. If a first name differs by one letter (e.g.
   Detlev/Detlef) and exactly one ranking line fits, the app still links them and reports it
   separately ("Abweichende Schreibweise übernommen — bitte prüfen"). Whoever still ended up at the
   end without a rank is listed in the report; correct the rest by hand with **▲▼**. Note: pressing
   "Setzliste aus Rangliste bilden" again replaces manual corrections with the ranking order.
5. The preview shows groups, number of games and goals per phase, e.g. "4 Gruppen (3×6er + 1×5er) ·
   55 Gruppenspiele 10/15/120".
6. Load and pick tables under **"3 · Tische"**, then **"🏁 Turnier erzeugen"**.

**Mode by number of starters:**

| Starters | Groups | Advance | Group goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24–32 | 8 (staircase system; the groups of 3 result from the last row) | groups of 4: 1st + 2nd; groups of 3: 1st directly, 2nd vs. 3rd (qualifier, 10/15/120) → round of 16, 10/20/120 | 32–30: 10/15/120 · 29–26: 10/20/120 · 25–24: 15/20/180 |
| 16–23 | 4 | 1st + 2nd → quarter-final | 23–21: 10/15/120 · 20–18: 10/20/120 · 17–16: 15/20/180 |
| 10–15 | 2 | 1st–4th → quarter-final | 15–14: 10/15/120 · 13–12: 10/20/120 · 11–10: 15/30/180 |

Quarter-final, semi-final and final: **15/30/180**. The DBU tender Grands Prix Kegel (V2,
2024-09-10) applies:

- **Groups** in a staircase system from the seeding list (§ 2.3 (1)). If more than two players of
  one club end up in a group, the app warns; swap by hand with ▲▼ (§ 2.3 (2)).
- **Group ranking:** match points (2/1/0), then average, then three-cushion points (§ 2.2 (3)). The
  average here is **own ÷ opponent's points**, not points ÷ innings.
- **KO seeded**, best against worst: quarter-final 1–8, 5–4, 3–6, 7–2 (§ 2.3 (4)–(5)), round of 16
  accordingly 1–16, 8–9, 5–12, 4–13, 3–14, 6–11, 7–10, 2–15. The seeding is shown as a table in the
  "Spiele & Gruppen" tab and in the final standings.
- **Changing the pairings of the first KO round:** in the "Spiele & Gruppen" tab, card
  "Platzierungs-/Finalrunde", choose under **"Paarungen"** between **"Gesetzt nach Rangliste"**
  (seeded by ranking) and **"Gruppen über Kreuz"** (groups crosswise; 4 groups: A1–B2, C1–D2 |
  B1–A2, D1–C2; 2 groups: A1–B4, B2–A3 | B1–A4, A2–B3; 8 groups accordingly, with the qualifier
  winner in place of the runner-up). Single players can be swapped per game; the card then shows
  "Von Hand geändert". This works **until the first KO game has started**.
- **Changing the pairings of later KO rounds** (quarter-final after the round of 16, semi-final): the
  same card shows **"Paarungen Halbfinale"** etc. per round, with a selection per slot. Whoever is
  chosen swaps places with the current player — winners only with winners. This works even before
  the winners are known, and **until a game of that round has been called**; again after
  „Ansetzen zurücknehmen“ (withdraw call). Changed games carry „Paarung geändert“ (pairing changed;
  the tooltip names where the player would have come from according to the plan);
  **„Paarung laut Plan“** (pairing as planned) reverts it. The final offers no selection — there is
  no alternative.
- **No third-place match**, third place shared. Places 5–8 (and 9–16) by the average of the lost
  KO game (§ 2.3 (6)).
- **Warm-up time** 3 minutes per player before every game (§ 5.1 (4)); the app sends it to the
  scoreboard.

The round of 16 or quarter-final becomes ready only once all group and qualifier games are
finished; on the second day the same tournament is continued.

**Game order from the DBU schedule:** the tender does not prescribe an order for the group games —
the organiser's schedule is binding. In the setup of the running tournament, card **"Spielfolge
(DBU-Zeitplan)"**, paste the game list and press **"↕ Spielfolge übernehmen"**. Easiest is straight
from the organiser's Excel file: in sheet **"Zeitplan"** select everything, copy, paste. What counts is the
**planned time** ("09:00 Uhr" below the game), not start and end; header, section and KO rows are
skipped. Without the Excel file, plain text works too, one game per line ("game time player – player",
e.g. "5 09:00 Muster – Beispiel"). The app matches each
line to a group game via the players and reports what it cannot match and which group games are
missing from the list. It then plays the group stage in this order: the next games are at the top,
one round is one time slot of the schedule. **"✕ Spielfolge entfernen"** restores the app's own order.

**During the tournament:**

1. "Spiele & Gruppen" tab: **"▶ Freie Tische belegen"** puts the next games on the scoreboards.
2. After every game **"📥 Ergebnis holen"**. Points, innings, high run and three-cushion points
   come from the scoreboard. By hand ("✎ Ergebnis erfassen"), enter the three-cushion points in
   "3B-Pkt".
3. **If the score sheet differs:** use **"✎ Korrigieren"** on the finished game. This works as long
   as no started or played follow-up game builds on the result — otherwise the app refuses and
   names the game. Group games can therefore be corrected until the KO stage starts, a KO game
   until the game of its winner starts. The old result is kept, the game is marked as "korrigiert";
   the scoreboard keeps the originally confirmed result.
4. **Back up after every round** ("Endstand" tab, "💾 Sicherung speichern") — see
   [Running a plan tournament](#fuehren).
5. **After a Carambus deploy, reload the app on all devices.** An open page otherwise keeps sending
   the old version.

**DBU online portal:** Carambus does not report the results to the portal (§ 2.4). The tournament
director enters them; the "📄 Spiele-CSV" in the "Endstand" tab helps with that.

The app sends the variant of the current phase with every game to the scoreboard; the
[Biathlon scoring](../players/biathlon.md) applies there.

!!! note "As of 2026-10-15"
    - The **qualifier rule for 24–32 starters** (groups of 3: 2nd vs. 3rd of the same group) is still
      a provisional decision; the tender does not regulate it. It plays no role at the GP on
      17–18 October (fewer than 20 starters).
    - The seeding list is matched to the ranking by **name** (the ranking has no DBU numbers).
      Reordering in the Carambus web therefore only affects players without a rank — make
      **manual corrections in the app**.

## After the tournament — the nightly run {#aufraeumen}

Every night at 0:01 Carambus cleans up after app tournaments on a club server:

- It releases the tables and closes the tournament monitor — **not** while the end date is in the future.
- It then deletes app tournaments with a closed monitor and a past end date, including their matches — **except**
  tournaments whose final standings are in the result archive.

In the „Endstand“ tab, **„🧹 Turnier in Carambus abräumen“** (clear tournament in Carambus) deletes the tournament and
its matches immediately, including the stored final standings. To keep a tournament, an end date in the future is
enough (admin: in the tournament's edit form).

## When something goes wrong {#stoerungen}

**The app no longer shows a tournament** — the browser has cleared its storage. Import the backup with „Sicherung
wiederherstellen“ (restore backup), open the app via the button or the link and sign in (signing in is not part of
the backup), open the restored tournament,
do not attach again. Without a backup, the results stored so far are in the result archive on the tournament page;
they do not come back into the app.

**The tables cannot be filled** („Table already in use“) — another tournament is still bound to the tables, usually a
test run where „➕ Neues Turnier“ was pressed instead of attaching. An admin can see in the console what is bound:

```ruby
TableMonitor.joins(:table).where(tables: {location_id: <location id>})
  .where.not(tournament_monitor_id: nil)
  .pluck(:id, :tournament_monitor_id, "tables.name")
```

**Resetting the tournament to the start** (admin, for example after a test run): tournament, seeding list and plan
stay; the matches played and the table bindings are removed.

```bash
cd /var/www/<basename>/current
DRY_RUN=1 RAILS_ENV=production bundle exec rake "external_tournament:reset_app_tournament[<tournament id>]"
RAILS_ENV=production bundle exec rake "external_tournament:reset_app_tournament[<tournament id>]"
```

The dry run lists the bound tables, the app games to be deleted and whether there are unconfirmed results among them —
entered at the scoreboard, never fetched by the app. Those would be lost. Afterwards use „🔄 Server-Stand neu laden“
(reload server state) in the app.

**The tournament has disappeared from Carambus** — check the end date; it is the only brake on the nightly run.
